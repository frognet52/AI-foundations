Module 1: Your AI assistant, used safely

What the holder can do

Use the right tool

Works in the school's approved AI assistant, signed in with a school account, rather than a personal app.

Protect pupils

Never puts names, SEN details or safeguarding information into AI. Uses "Pupil A" and general descriptions instead.

Stay the professional

Reads, checks and edits every AI draft for accuracy, tone and suitability before it is used.

How it is earned

  1. Watch Module 1. A two-minute video covering the three safe-use rules and three everyday tasks.
  2. Pass the knowledge check. Three scenario questions, all answered correctly.
  3. Submit one real resource. Something adapted with the school's approved AI tool, with no pupil data, plus a one-line note on what was checked or changed.
